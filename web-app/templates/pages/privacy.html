Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Sorcerers Summit, we collect the following information:

  • Discord Account Information: When you log in via Discord OAuth, we collect your Discord user ID, username, and avatar to display your profile and match history.
  • Match Data: We store match results, deck information, and ELO ratings that you report through the Discord bot or web application.
  • Usage Data: We collect basic analytics about how you use the website, including pages visited and features used.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Maintain ELO rankings and leaderboards
  • Display match history and statistics
  • Provide personalized features like player profiles
  • Improve the service and add new features
  • Communicate with you about the service

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be visible to other users in the following ways:

  • Your username and avatar appear on public leaderboards
  • Your match history is visible to other logged-in users
  • Your deck information may be visible if you participate in tournaments

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to record-keeping requirements)
  • Opt-out of data collection by not using the service

6. Cookies

We use session cookies to maintain your login state when you authenticate via Discord. These cookies are essential for the service to function properly.

7. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Discord: For authentication and bot functionality
  • Curiosa API: For card and deck data retrieval

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via Discord or the website.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via Discord: Join our Discord server and reach out to OFN-CJ or Goldeneagle.
