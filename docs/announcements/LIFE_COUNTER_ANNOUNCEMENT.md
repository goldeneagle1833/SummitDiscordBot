# 📱 New Feature: Mobile Life Counter + Match Reporting!

Hey Sorcerers! We've launched a **mobile-optimized life counter** with integrated match reporting! 🎉

## 🎯 Quick Guide

### **Access It**
Visit: **sorcererssummit.com/life-counter** on your phone
*(Or tap the life counter icon in the navbar)*

### **Track Your Game**
- Tap **+/−** buttons to adjust life totals
- Track threshold (mana) counters for all 4 elements
- State auto-saves if you refresh!

### **Report Match Results**
When someone hits 0 life:
1. Tap **"📝 Report Match"**
2. Search for your opponent
3. Paste your Curiosa deck URL
4. Select who went first
5. Click **"I Won"** or **"I Lost"**

### **Confirm Matches**
When opponents report against you:
- Modal pops up automatically
- Review the details
- Click **Confirm** or **Deny**
- ELO updates instantly! ⚡

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Auto-saves your progress** - refresh-safe!
- **Fullscreen mode** - tap ⛶ for immersive play
- **48-hour confirmation window** - auto-confirms if no response
- **Recent opponent search** - find opponents fast
- **Dual-sided display** - hand phone to opponent, top is rotated 180°

---

## 📋 Requirements
- **Login required** to report matches (Discord or Google)
- **Deck URL required** for match reports (submitter only)

---

## 💡 Pro Tips
- Use fullscreen (⛶) for distraction-free games
- Submit reports right after your match ends
- Check pending confirmations regularly
- Deck URLs help track the meta!
