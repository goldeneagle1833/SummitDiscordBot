How to Use Sorcerers Summit

Getting Started

Sorcerers Summit is a community-driven platform for Sorcery: Contested Realm players. Join our Discord server to start reporting matches and participating in the ELO leaderboard.

Reporting Matches

Use the Discord bot commands to report match results:

  • /report - Report a match result
  • /lfg - Find other players looking for games
  • /mystats - Check your personal statistics

ELO System

Our ELO rating system tracks player skill over time. Win matches to increase your rating and climb the leaderboard!

Need More Help?

Join our Discord server and ask in the support channel, or reach out to the admins (OFN-CJ or Goldeneagle).
