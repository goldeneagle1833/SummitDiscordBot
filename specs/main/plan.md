# Implementation Plan: Daily Summary Message

**Branch**: `main` | **Date**: 2026-04-07 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)
**Input**: Feature specification from `specs/main/spec.md`

## Summary

Add an automated daily summary cog that posts a rich embed to a Discord channel at 11:30 PM EST. The embed shows the day's competitive stats: total matches, most active player, ELO swings, top gainer/loser, biggest upset, hot streaks, broken streaks, and average match duration. Stats are passed through GPT (`gpt-4.1-nano`) to generate an entertaining commentary paragraph at the top of the embed. Purely read-only — queries existing `match_records` table, no schema changes.
